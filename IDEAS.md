## Plex wrapped

Year-in-review for Plex watch history, exactly like Spotify Wrapped.
Pull plays from Plex (or Tautulli) and summarize: top shows and movies,
total hours, busiest days, genres, and who in the household watched what.

Origin: Stephen thought it would be funny to see how many times they
watched Cars. That's the headline stat.
